Quesillo

Quesillo is a traditional Nicaraguan cheese dish made with fresh cheese and served with a variety of accompaniments.

Description:

Quesillo is a delicious and simple cheese dish that is a staple in Nicaraguan cuisine. It is often served as a snack or appetizer and is known for its creamy texture and mild flavor.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of fresh cheese (queso fresco)
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a separate pan, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté until soft.
  2. Add the fresh cheese to the pan. Stir gently until the cheese is warm and slightly melted.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook for an additional 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy your Quesillo!
