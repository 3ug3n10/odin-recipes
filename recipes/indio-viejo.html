Indio Viejo

Indio Viejo is a traditional Nicaraguan dish made with beef and served with a variety of accompaniments.

Description:

Indio Viejo is a hearty and flavorful dish that showcases the rich culinary traditions of Nicaragua. The dish is known for its tender beef and savory accompaniments.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb of beef, cut into strips
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a separate pan, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté until soft.
  2. Add the beef strips to the pan. Cook until the meat is browned and cooked through.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook for an additional 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy your Indio Viejo!
