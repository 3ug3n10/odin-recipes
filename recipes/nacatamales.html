Nacatamales

Nacatamales are traditional Nicaraguan tamales wrapped in banana leaves. They are a beloved dish often served during special occasions and family gatherings.

Description:

Nacatamales are made with masa (corn dough) and filled with a variety of ingredients, including meat, beans, and vegetables. The mixture is wrapped in banana leaves and steamed to perfection, resulting in a flavorful and satisfying dish.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of masa (corn dough)
  • 1 cup of chicken or pork, shredded
  • 1 cup of black beans, cooked
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a separate pan, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and garlic. Sauté until the vegetables are soft.
  2. Add the shredded meat and black beans to the pan. Stir well to combine.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook for an additional 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Spread the masa on a flat surface and place a portion of the filling in the center.
  5. Fold the masa over the filling to create a sealed packet.
  6. Wrap each packet in a banana leaf.
  7. Cook the nacatamales in a steamer for about 30 minutes or until fully cooked.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your Nacatamales!
